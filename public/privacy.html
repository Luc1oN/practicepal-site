Privacy

Last updated: July 2026 · Applies to the PracticePal app and practicepal.ie

The short version: PracticePal works without an account, collects as little as possible, shows no ads, uses no third-party trackers, and never sells anything about you to anyone.

Who runs PracticePal

PracticePal is built and run by an individual developer in Cork, Ireland. For anything privacy-related, email practicepal.cork@gmail.com.

Using the app without an account

You can generate and run sessions without signing up. When you do, we process:

  • Your session settings (players, courts, duration, level, and any focus text you type) — sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, to generate your plan. Don't put personal details in the focus box; it never needs any.
  • A generation log — each generated session is stored for improving the product (for example, learning which kinds of sessions people actually create). It isn't tied to your name or email.
  • An anonymous device identifier — a random ID stored in your browser, used only to enforce fair daily usage limits. It says nothing about who you are.
  • Preferences on your device — things like your last-used settings and display theme live only in your own browser's storage, not on our servers.

If you create an account

  • We store your email address and a securely hashed password, managed by Supabase (our database provider).
  • Your saved sessions, favourites, and notes are stored against your account so they follow you across devices. Only you can see them.

Sharing and feedback

  • If you share a session link, that session becomes viewable by anyone who has the link. Don't share sessions containing anything you'd rather keep private.
  • If you submit post-session feedback (the drill ratings), it's stored for product improvement and isn't shown publicly.
  • If you join the beta on practicepal.ie, we store the name, email, and optional club/answers you give — used only to run the beta and contact you about it.

Where your data lives

All data is stored with Supabase in the EU (Ireland, AWS eu-west-1). Session generation uses Anthropic's Claude API to produce your plan; only the session settings above are sent for that purpose.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no advertising trackers.
  • No third-party analytics scripts.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone for their own purposes.
  • No marketing emails you didn't ask for.

Your rights

You can ask at any time to see what we hold about you, or to have your account and its data deleted. Email practicepal.cork@gmail.com from the address on your account and it'll be handled promptly — there's no self-serve delete button yet, so during the beta this is a manual (but fast) process.

Changes

If this policy changes in any meaningful way, the date at the top changes with it, and anything significant will be flagged in the app or by email to beta participants.
